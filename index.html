Maintenance Tips

🌬️

Change HVAC Filters Every 90 Days

A clogged filter forces your system to work harder, reducing efficiency by up to 15% and shortening unit lifespan.

🔥

Test Smoke Detectors Monthly

Most detector failures occur due to dead batteries or dust buildup. A 30-second monthly test could save your life.

💧

Inspect Water Heater Annually

Flushing sediment buildup annually can extend your water heater's life by up to 5 years and improve efficiency.
